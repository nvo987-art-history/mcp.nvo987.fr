MCP Interpretive Infrastructure is an infrastructure layer
within the NEXARIUM Civilisation Layer.

It provides structured and standardized connectivity
between artificial intelligence models, intelligent agents,
tools, resources, and external systems through MCP.

Its purpose is to make context, resources, and operational
capabilities accessible and interpretable for machine systems.

It is not an AI model or a data repository.

It is a machine interpretation and connectivity infrastructure.
